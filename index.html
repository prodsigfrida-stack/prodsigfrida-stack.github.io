BASS HARMONIZER

BASS HARMONIZER is a free bass pitch-harmonizer plugin. Put it on a bass, 808, or low-end track and it adds a second voice at a pitch you choose — mix, harmonics, microshift, distortion, reverb, and vibrato sit on that harmony.

  • Pitch harmony in semitone steps and octaves, with a wet/dry mix
  • Harmonics, microshift width, distortion, reverb, and vibrato on the pitched voice
  • Stable Pitch mode for cleaner tracking, mono below 250 Hz, and autogain
  • Free insert effect — VST3 on Windows, VST3 and Audio Unit on Mac

Windows x64 VST3 · macOS universal VST3 + AU · Ableton, FL Studio, Logic, Reaper, GarageBand
