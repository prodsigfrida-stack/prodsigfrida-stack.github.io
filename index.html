BASS HARMONIZER

by sigfrida

A free bass pitch-harmonizer. Put it on a bass, 808, or low-end track and it adds a second voice at a pitch you choose.

  • Pitch steps and octaves, with a wet/dry mix
  • Harmonics, microshift, distortion, reverb, vibrato
  • Stable Pitch, mono below 250 Hz, autogain
  • VST3 on Windows · VST3 + AU on Mac

Ableton, FL Studio, Logic, Reaper, GarageBand

SIGFRIDA is an electronic/pop music producer, DJ, and audio engineer based in Buenos Aires, Argentina. Known for producing, mixing, and mastering within the Latin electronic, and experimental club scenes, his work blends electronic bass lines, heavy sampling, and energetic house music. @fridalust

Download

Windows Mac

Windows: copy the BASS HARMONIZER.vst3 folder (the whole VST3 bundle, not a single file) into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\. You may need to allow Administrator access. If your plugins live on another drive (for example D:), copy that folder into the location your DAW actually scans.
Mac (Ableton, FL, Reaper…): drop the .vst3 folder into ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/ or /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/. Logic / GarageBand: drop the .component folder into ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/ or /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
Then restart your DAW or rescan plug-ins.
